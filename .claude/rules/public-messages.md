# Messages other people read

For drafts that land in shared places: pull-request and issue comments, tracker comments, chat, shared pages. Commit messages and your own notes are excluded.

1. **Label it.** The first line reads `Drafted with an AI assistant.` *Why: people should know how a message was produced before they act on it.*
2. **Mark how each claim was checked.** Tag every finding with one of `[tested]` (a test or reproduction ran), `[read]` (code was read, nothing ran) or `[guess]`. *Why: "looks thorough" is not evidence; the tag is.*
3. **Let the user cut.** Show the numbered list of findings first and drop the ones the user rejects. *Why: the person whose name is on the post decides what it says.*
4. **Keep it short.** Lead with the conclusion, give `file:line` or steps to reproduce, and paste no logs that may hold credentials or personal data. *Why: posts are hard to retract and long ones go unread.*
5. **Approve the final draft.** Post only after the user has seen the final text; any edit after that needs another look. *Why: approval of a draft is not approval of a different text.*
